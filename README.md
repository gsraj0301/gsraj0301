## 🌱 Where it started

### [GST Reconciler](https://github.com/gsraj0301/gst-reconciler): my first real automation

My dad is an accountant, and every GST cycle he had to match his **Tally** purchase register against the **GSTR-2B** from the GST portal, invoice by invoice. That took him **2 to 4 hours**. So I built him a desktop app. He drops in two Excel files, and it matches invoices by GSTIN, amount and invoice number, flags every mismatch in IGST, CGST and SGST, and gives back a colour-coded report without touching his original file. It now takes **5 minutes at most**.
